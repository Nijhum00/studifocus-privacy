Delete Your Account

If you would like to delete your StudiFocus account and all associated data, please send us a deletion request using the button below.

What will be deleted

  • Your account (name and email address)
  • All study sessions and history
  • Your goals, streaks, and subjects
  • All cloud-backed data stored in Firebase

How to request deletion

Send an email to info@nijhumtech.com with the subject line "Account Deletion Request" and include the Gmail address associated with your StudiFocus account.

We will permanently delete your account and data within 30 days.

Request Account Deletion

Note: Deletion is permanent and cannot be undone. Local data stored on your device must be removed by uninstalling the app.
